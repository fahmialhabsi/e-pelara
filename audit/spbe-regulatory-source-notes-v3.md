## Use in the audit

The final matrix must distinguish direct SPBE obligations from supporting technical controls, related regulations, and internal project policies. A source status of `Berlaku` does not prove implementation in e-PeLARA. The project must provide design, implementation, automated-test, operational, and independent-verifier evidence before using a compliance status stronger than `Not Verifiable` or `Partially Compliant`.

## Limitations

This note verifies public catalogue/status pages and high-level descriptions. It does not replace reading the official full text and annexes for article-level control mapping. Article-level applicability and local-government policy alignment remain `Not Verifiable` until the official texts, local policies, and owner decisions are attached and reviewed.
